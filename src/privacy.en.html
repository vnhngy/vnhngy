2. Your entries stay on your device

Everything you enter in __APP__ is stored only in the app's storage on your device: cars (e.g. number plate, VIN, photos), fill-ups, service, costs, deadlines, defects, emergency numbers, accident reports with location and photos, receipt photos, favourite stations, ratings, discounts, price alerts and settings. This data is not transferred to us or to third parties. Backups made by the app are also kept only on your device.

When you export a file (backup, spreadsheet, calendar or PDF), you decide where it goes using your device's share menu. If you delete the app or tap “Delete all my data”, this data is removed. We do not keep a copy.

3. Fuel prices for your area

To show fuel prices near you, the app sends the following to our server: a random device ID created on first launch, which is not linked to your name; the place you enter (postcode or place name, or your location's coordinates if you tap “My location”); the radius; your work place and a travel destination if you enter them; the time of last use.

The server converts place names into coordinates. For this it sends only the place name you entered to the Nominatim service of the OpenStreetMap Foundation, not your device ID and not your IP address. Prices are then fetched for a rough area around your place. Price data and requested areas are not linked to you.

Purpose: showing prices near you. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. If you do not use the app for 7 days, the server stops fetching prices for you; after 60 days without use, the place and device ID are deleted automatically. You can change the place in the app at any time or remove it immediately with “Delete all my data”.

4. Connection to the server

The app loads prices, opening hours, weather and leasing offers from our server. Our hosting provider processes technically necessary connection data (IP address, time, requested address) in logs that are deleted after a short time. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (secure and stable operation).

The hosting provider is Supabase Inc. with servers in the EU (Ireland). Supabase processes the data on our behalf under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR).

5. Device features

Location: only when you use “My location” or the accident report and have allowed it. For prices near you, the coordinates are sent to the server as described in section 3; in the accident report they stay on the device.

Camera and photos: only when you take or choose a photo and have allowed it. Photos stay on the device.

6. Other services

  • Fuel prices: German Market Transparency Unit for Fuels (MTS-K) via Tankerkönig (CC BY 4.0). Prices are fetched by our server, not by your device.
  • Map: the map in the app uses OpenStreetMap data (ODbL) included in the app. No data is transferred for it.
  • Navigation and links: if you tap “Apple Maps”, “Google Maps” or a link to an offer, the respective app or website opens and its privacy policy applies from then on.
  • PDF: the service log and accident report are created directly on your device.
  • Feedback by email: if you write to us, we process your email address and message to reply (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and delete them when no longer needed.
  • App Store and Google Play: downloads and updates are handled by Apple or Google under their privacy policies. We only receive aggregated statistics that do not identify individuals.
